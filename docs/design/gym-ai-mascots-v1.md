# Gym&AI mascot concepts

Three proposed companions replace the octopus direction. This is visual exploration, not a final character selection or a production 3D model.

| Name | Primary goal | Character direction | Suggested motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Atlas | Get stronger | Grounded cyan pebble with a broad rounded silhouette; steady and encouraging | Small nods and deliberate arm gestures |
| Flex | Build muscle | Springy coral bean; focused and upbeat | Gentle stretch and a celebratory bounce |
| Pip | Feel fitter | Buoyant lime pear/spark; welcoming and playful | Small hops and relaxed breathing |

All three share soft plush materials, simple eyes, and short limbs. Readiness, coaching, and resting expressions should remain recognizable at small sizes. Use words alongside the character to explain workout status; expression and color alone should never carry essential information.

Recommended family: these three abstract creatures suit the app's minimal layout. Pip is the proposed general-purpose welcome character, while onboarding assigns Atlas or Flex when those goals are selected. Let users change their companion. Each companion provides the same core coaching capabilities; personality and emphasis vary.

The visual references are the approachable companion feeling of Meta Muse and OpenAI Dots. Silhouettes, faces, and character details are original proposals for Gym&AI.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The exact generation prompt is saved in `gym-ai-mascots-v1.prompt.txt`. The concept board is `gym-ai-mascots-v1.png`.

Visual review: names, goals, and expression labels are readable and correctly spelled. All three main characters and expression rows are visible. Colors and plush materials are consistent. No octopus features appear. Expression variants are concept sketches; final production assets will need stricter silhouette consistency, particularly Flex's resting pose.

Next production step after selection: individual transparent renders and a small set of consistent animation states. This board does not supply a rig, mesh, or animation assets.
